# The Velloran Glasswork Guild — Apprentice Grading Code (fictional guild)

The Velloran Glasswork Guild is invented for this study. Nothing below describes a real guild,
trade body, standard or law. About half the rules deliberately run against intuitions from real
crafts and workplaces, so a solver cannot answer from general knowledge.

## Grades
- G1. Apprentices progress through four grades: Ash, Cinder, Flame and Ember. Ember is the lowest grade and Ash the highest.
- G2. An apprentice may skip a grade only by passing two consecutive assessments at the higher grade's standard.
- G3. A grade lapses after 18 months without a logged piece, and the apprentice drops two grades, not one.

## Assessments
- A1. Each assessment piece is judged by three masters; the piece passes if any two agree, but the lowest mark is the one recorded.
- A2. A piece that cracks within 24 hours of assessment is failed, even if it had already passed.
- A3. An apprentice may withdraw a piece before marking only once per grade; a second withdrawal counts as a fail.

## Furnace and workshop
- F1. Only Flame grade or higher may light a furnace; any grade may tend one already lit.
- F2. A furnace must be shut down by the person who lit it, unless a master signs the log in their place.
- F3. The workshop log is kept in pencil, and corrections are made by adding a new line, never by erasing.

## Materials
- M1. Coloured glass belongs to the guild, not the apprentice, until a piece made from it passes assessment.
- M2. Recycled cullet may make up at most a third of an assessment piece; more than that is legal only in Ash-grade pieces.
- M3. Gold leaf may be used only in the final assessment of a grade, never in earlier ones.

## Fees and dues
- D1. Assessment fees are paid after the result, and only for passed pieces.
- D2. Dues are halved for any year in which the apprentice teaches at least ten sessions to a lower grade.
- D3. An apprentice who owes dues may still be assessed, but a pass is not recorded until the dues are paid.

## Conduct and disputes
- C1. A complaint about a master is heard by the two most junior masters, not the senior ones.
- C2. A disputed mark may be appealed within 7 days; an appeal that fails lowers the recorded mark by one band.
- C3. An apprentice found using another's piece is suspended for one grade period and keeps their current grade.

## Seniority
- K1. Among apprentices of the same grade, seniority goes to the one who reached that grade later, not earlier.
- K2. Seniority decides furnace booking order on weekdays only; weekend bookings go by drawing lots.
- K3. An apprentice who drops a grade keeps their old seniority within the lower grade.
