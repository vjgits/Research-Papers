# Tarnball — League Rulebook (fictional sport)

Tarnball is an invented sport created for this study. Nothing below describes a real sport or
league. About half the rules deliberately run against intuitions from real ball games, so a solver
cannot answer from general sports knowledge.

## The ring
- G1. The pitch is a ring, not a rectangle. Both teams attack the same central goal, called the Tarn.
- G2. A ball that leaves the ring is put back into play by the team that touched it last, not by their opponents.
- G3. The inner circle around the Tarn (the Shallows) may hold at most one player from each team at a time.

## Scoring
- S1. Sinking the ball in the Tarn scores 2 points; sinking it on a rebound off the rim scores 3.
- S2. A goal in the last 60 seconds of a period counts double, but only for the team that is behind when it is scored.
- S3. A team that leads by 15 points or more must play one player short until its lead falls below 10.
- S4. Own goals count for neither team; instead, the team that did not touch the ball last gains possession.

## Players and possession
- P1. Each team fields 6 players. One must be the Warden, who may not score.
- P2. A player may hold the ball for at most 5 seconds. Holding it longer gives the opponents a free throw, not possession.
- P3. Passing the ball away from the Tarn is legal only for the Warden.

## Fouls
- O1. Contact with the ball carrier's arms is a foul; shoulder-to-shoulder contact with the body is legal.
- O2. After a foul, the fouled team chooses either a 2-minute sit-out for the fouling player or a free throw, never both.
- O3. A player's third foul in a match sends off that player's captain, not the player.

## Timing
- T1. A match has three periods of 14 minutes.
- T2. The clock never stops, even for injuries. Lost time is added only to the third period.
- T3. A period ends at the first dead ball after the horn, not at the horn itself.

## Officials and challenges
- R1. Each team has two challenges per match. A successful challenge is kept; an unsuccessful one costs the team 1 point.
- R2. Inside the Shallows, the ring referee's call overrides the video official; everywhere else, the video official's call stands.

## Equipment
- E1. The ball is weighed before each period. If it is found underweight, goals scored with it in the previous period are void.
- E2. Players may wear gloves only in the third period.

## Substitutions
- U1. Substitutions are allowed only straight after a goal.
- U2. A substituted player may return once, and only in place of the player who replaced them.

## Tiebreaks and standings
- K1. A tied match is decided by Warden throws: the Wardens throw in turn until one misses.
- K2. In the league table a win earns 3 points, a tiebreak win 1 point and a tiebreak loss 2 points.
- K3. Teams level on table points are ranked by fewer goals scored, not more.
