# The Corvane Harbor Code — Trader's Reference (fictional)

The Corvane Harbor Code is an invented body of port law created for this study. Nothing below
describes a real jurisdiction. About half the rules deliberately run against everyday intuition about
trade, fairness or bureaucracy, so a solver cannot answer from general knowledge.

## Berths
- B1. Berths are assigned by hull length, not by arrival order. The longest waiting hull gets the next free berth.
- B2. A vessel may hold a berth for at most 4 tides. On the 5th tide it must leave, even mid-unloading.
- B3. Vessels flying a mourning pennant may not be refused a berth, but pay double berth fees.

## Tariffs
- T1. Tariffs are charged on cargo leaving the harbor, not cargo arriving.
- T2. Salt, iron and glass are tariff-free; every other cargo pays one-tenth of its declared value.
- T3. Under-declaring value is not punished by a fine; the harbor may instead buy the cargo at the declared value.

## Pilots
- P1. Every vessel over 30 paces long must take a harbor pilot to enter, but never to leave.
- P2. A pilot's fee is paid by the vessel that berthed nearest to the incoming one, not by the incoming vessel.
- P3. A pilot may refuse a vessel only during fog; refusal at any other time voids the pilot's licence.

## Disputes
- D1. Disputes between traders are heard by the Tide Court, which sits only at low tide.
- D2. The party that files second is heard first.
- D3. A Tide Court ruling can be appealed once, to the harbormaster, who may only uphold or void it, never change it.

## Warehouses
- W1. Goods stored for more than 9 days become property of the warehouse unless a renewal token is lodged.
- W2. Renewal tokens extend storage by 9 days and cost the same whatever the volume stored.
- W3. Perishable goods may not be renewed; they must be sold or destroyed by day 9.

## Contracts
- C1. A spoken contract is binding only if made within sight of the harbor bell.
- C2. Written contracts must be witnessed by one person who is not a trader.
- C3. A contract signed during a festival day is void unless both parties re-affirm it within 3 days.

## Crews
- K1. Crew members may not disembark until the cargo master has signed the manifest.
- K2. A crew member who stays ashore past three bells is counted as a deserter, but the vessel, not the crew member, is fined.

## Lights and signals
- L1. A red lamp at the masthead means "carrying no cargo"; a green lamp means "carrying tariff-free cargo".
- L2. A vessel showing no lamp at night is presumed to carry taxable cargo and is inspected first.

## Penalties
- X1. Fines are paid in the cargo the vessel carries, never in coin.
- X2. A second offence within a year doubles the fine; a third bars the vessel for one season.
